# Laptop requests, replacements, and battery service

Standard issue is a MacBook Pro or a Dell Latitude, refreshed on a 3-year cycle. All devices ship zero-touch: they enroll and configure themselves on first boot.

## New hire laptops

Managers request hardware through the onboarding form at least 7 business days before the start date. Devices arrive configured; the new hire signs in with Okta on first boot and everything else is automatic. Late requests are fulfilled from the local buffer stock when available, but 7 days is the promise we can keep.

## Replacement for a slow or failing laptop

If your device is past its 3-year refresh date, request a refresh through the IT portal and a replacement ships within a week, no justification needed. Inside the refresh window, open a ticket describing the issue; hardware faults (battery, keyboard, screen) go to repair or swap depending on parts availability.

## Battery draining fast

First check Activity Monitor (Mac) or Task Manager (Windows) for an app pinning the CPU; browser tabs with video are the usual culprit. If the battery health reading shows below 80 percent capacity, open a ticket with a screenshot of the battery health screen; batteries below 80 percent qualify for service without further diagnostics.
