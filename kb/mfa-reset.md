# MFA factors: new phone, lost phone, stolen phone

Your MFA factor is the key to your whole account. Treat a lost factor as urgent.

## Lost or stolen phone

This is the urgent path. Call the IT help desk line or ask a teammate to flag IT immediately so your MFA factor can be revoked; until then, whoever holds the phone can approve pushes as you. Deny any MFA prompt you did not trigger. After the factor is revoked, IT verifies your identity on a video call before enrolling a replacement factor.

## New phone, old phone still in hand

Self-service. Sign in to Okta from your laptop, open Settings, then Security Methods, and add the new phone as a factor while the old one still works. Once the new factor is verified, remove the old phone. Doing it in this order avoids the identity-verification step entirely.

## New phone, old phone already wiped or traded in

If you kept the same number, SMS verification may still work for enrollment; try adding a factor from Okta Settings first. If you cannot verify at all, contact IT for identity verification on a video call, after which a fresh factor enrollment is sent to your new phone.
