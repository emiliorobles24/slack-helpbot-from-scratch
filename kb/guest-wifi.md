# Guest Wi-Fi for visitors

Visitors never join the corporate network. The guest network is internet-only and isolated from all internal systems.

## Get a visitor online

Connect the visitor's device to the network named "Guest". The daily password rotates and is printed on the card at each reception desk; front desk staff can also read it out. Guest sessions last 24 hours and then re-prompt.

## Hosting a large meeting or event

For more than 10 visitors, ask IT at least 2 business days ahead for an event passcode, which supports up to 200 devices for the duration of the event and avoids everyone queueing at reception for the daily password.

## Why visitors cannot use the corporate SSID

The corporate network requires a company-managed device with a certificate; personal and visitor devices cannot join it even with an employee's credentials. This is by design: it keeps unmanaged devices away from internal systems. Contractors who need internal access get a managed device through their sponsoring manager.
