# Office printers

Every floor has one multifunction printer, named by floor: PRINT-2, PRINT-3, PRINT-4. All support print, scan-to-email, and badge release.

## Add a printer to your laptop

Managed laptops get printers automatically through the self-service app store: open it, search for your floor's printer, and click install. Do not add printers by IP address manually; those bypass badge release and break after maintenance.

## Nothing comes out when I print

Jobs only print after you badge in at the printer within 4 hours of sending (badge release keeps sensitive documents off the output tray). Walk to the printer, tap your badge, and select your job. If the job is not listed at the printer: confirm you printed to the floor printer and not a PDF destination, check the printer screen for a paper or toner message, then reinstall the printer from self-service. Still stuck, open a ticket and include the printer name and the document type.

## Scanning

At the printer, badge in and choose Scan to Email; the scan goes to your own inbox and cannot be misdirected. For scans over 50 pages use the document feeder in batches of 50.
